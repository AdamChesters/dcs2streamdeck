Hold: the switch springs back when you let go. Set: it stays. Turn: a short press is a small step; hold to turn faster.
Untick any you don't want a press to land on. None ticked = all listed.
Matches appear in the list as you type; click one, or press Enter for the top match. The text shows the switch; the highlight shows this light. Filled in for you when the switch has its own light. A light with several colours (L BAR) or parts (AOA indexer) shows all of them. Held and magnetic switches stay lit while on and go dark when the aircraft releases them.
